# Support FAQ – VACUUM HEROES

**Q: How do I restore purchases?**  
A: Settings → Restore Purchases (or store account restores automatically).

**Q: How do I cancel VIP?**  
A: Only via App Store / Google Play subscription management.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Email support@… with your player ID. Deletion within 30 days.

**Q: Game crashed / bug**  
A: Email support with device model, OS version, screenshot, steps.

**Q: Not enough energy**  
A: Wait for regen (1 per 6 min), watch rewarded ad, or use gems.
